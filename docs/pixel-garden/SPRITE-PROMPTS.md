# 四季植物图片生成记录
## Weekly Garden：侧视生长图集

使用内置 image_gen 工具生成，同季月视图素材仅作为画风与配色参考。植物单独生成，土壤仍由场景绘制。保留原始透明 PNG；读取透明边界记录各阶段的 SVG 显示区域，没有重新裁剪或变色。

## Monthly Garden：俯视生长图集

使用内置 image_gen 工具生成，再通过同一工具进行仅配色的编辑；保留真实透明背景。每季一张 2×2 图集，依次为萌芽、小灌木、成长期和成熟期。布局使用 SVG viewBox 按透明边界选取，未对生成图片做手工裁剪或变色。参考的是俯视像素园艺的风格特征，所有素材独立生成。

## spring

### 初稿提示词

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production pixel-art botanical sprite atlas for a small blog contribution garden. Create ONE square 1024 by 1024 PNG sprite sheet with true transparent background, a precisely aligned 2 by 2 grid of four invisible 512 by 512 cells. Each cell contains exactly ONE isolated top-down plant sprite, centered at respectively (256,256), (768,256), (256,768), (768,768). Cell order: upper left young sprout (two leaves, occupies about 120 by 120 pixels), upper right small growing shrub (about 210 by 210), lower left intermediate canopy (about 300 by 300), lower right mature canopy (about 380 by 380). All four must be visibly distinct growth stages of the same seasonal species, increasing foliage density, size and detail. Keep generous clear transparent margins within EACH cell; all plant pixels must stay inside their own cell; no touching between sprites. Style: original 1990s handheld game / cozy farming game pixel art, strict straight overhead view of foliage only, small hard-edged square pixel clusters, restrained 8-12 color palette, dark irregular contour, directional upper-left pixel highlights, layered dark leaf pockets, delicate densely hand-placed stipple highlights. Designed to read well at tiny sizes. Pixel-unit about 6-8 source pixels; no smooth vector shapes, no airbrushed gradients, no painted brush strokes, no perspective trunks or side view. A minimal crisp pixel contact shadow directly beneath each plant is allowed. No soil, ground tiles, floor, scene, checkerboard, labels, letters, text, borders, divider lines, watermark or UI. Draw independently original foliage silhouettes and textures, using only general genre characteristics. Seasonal direction: Cherry blossom shrub, distinct irregular cloud-like clusters of five-petal pink flowers rather than a single ball. Several overlapping rounded but lumpy blossom tufts and small deep green leaves at the edges. Pale blush highlights, dusty rose midtones, plum shaded gaps, occasional tiny warm cream flower centers. Growth stage 1 is a small pink-tipped green sprout; stage 2 is a young leafy shrub with just a few pink buds; stage 3 has several separate blossom clusters; stage 4 is a lush asymmetrical multi-lobed pink cherry blossom crown.
